```json
    "specific scene or event that shifts their perspective (3-5 total)",

  "final_state": "who they become by the end—may vary based on player choices, should show meaningful growth",

}
```

**Character Creation Guidelines:**

**Names:** Fit setting (contemporary/fantasy/etc.), memorable, distinct from each other

**Initial States:** Clear personality/worldview, hint at strengths and flaws, set up potential for growth

**Key Turning Points:** Specific scenes/events, progressive change, connect to story arc, challenge existing worldview

**Final States:** Earned by journey (not arbitrary), allow variation based on player choices, show meaningful change

**Emotional Trajectories:** Capture inner journey (not just external events), identify core emotional conflict/growth
