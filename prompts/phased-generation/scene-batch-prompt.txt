## SCENE STRUCTURE

Each scene needs:
- **index**: Must match the outline index exactly
- **title**: Keep from outline (may refine slightly)
- **phase**: Keep from outline
- **purpose**: Keep from outline (may refine slightly)
- **situation**: 1-2 sentences describing what the protagonists face
- **key_events_if_unchallenged**: 3-5 core events that advance the scene
- **choice_points**: 0-2 per scene (story-altering decisions only)
- **character_focus**: Array with `name`, `emotional_beat_target`, and `turning_point` fields
- **beats**: 3-7 beats marking progression through the scene
- **hooks_for_future**: Threads connecting to later scenes

Beat types: **establishment** (set scene/mood) | **hook** (create interest) | **reaction** (character response) | **escalation** (raise stakes) | **pivot** (change direction) | **emotional** (emotional moment) | **transition** (bridge to next scene)

Each beat requires: index, title, description, type, and required (true/false).

**character_focus** format:


```json

  }
]
```
