## SCENE COHESION RULES

A scene is a **complete dramatic unit**. Do NOT split continuous action into separate scenes.

**Anti-Split Rule:** Keep together:
- Action sequences and their immediate emotional aftermath
- A crisis and its resolution when they occur in the same location
- A confrontation and its direct consequences if they flow without interruption

**Climax-Resolution Unity:** The CLIMAX phase scene and its immediate aftermath MUST be combined if they occur in the same location, less than 1 hour passes, and the same core characters are involved.
