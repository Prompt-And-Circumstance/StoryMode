## SCENE STRUCTURE

Each scene needs:
- **situation**: 1–2 sentences describing what the protagonists face
- **key_events_if_unchallenged**: 3–5 core events that advance the scene (player choices override/expand these)
- **choice_points**: 0–2 per scene (story-altering decisions only; include example player intents and narrative consequences)
- **beats**: 3–7 beats that mark progression through the scene (see beat types below)

Beat types: **establishment** (set scene/mood) | **hook** (create interest) | **reaction** (character response) | **escalation** (raise stakes) | **pivot** (change direction) | **emotional** (emotional moment) | **transition** (bridge to next scene)

Each beat requires: index, title, description, type, and required (true/false).

Optional: character_focus (which characters experience emotional beats), hooks_for_future (threads connecting to later scenes), expected_rounds (how many rounds this scene spans).

**CRITICAL:** Ensure that the characters' **Key Turning Points** (listed in the CHARACTERS section) are represented in the scene plan. Assign specific scenes to trigger these pivotal moments.
