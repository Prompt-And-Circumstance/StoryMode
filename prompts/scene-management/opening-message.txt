## Your Task

Write the **opening narrative message** that establishes Scene 1 for the player. This is the story's first impression.

### Requirements:

1. **Establish the Scene**: Use the situation above to set up Scene 1. Make the player feel immediately immersed in the world.

2. **Create Atmosphere**: Match the setting's atmosphere ({{ATMOSPHERE}}) and the story's primary tone ({{TONE_PRIMARY}}).

3. **Introduce Key Elements**: 
   - Hint at the core premise without explaining everything
   - Suggest the antagonistic forces ({{ANTAGONIST_NATURE}})

   - Set up the opening hook ({{ARC_OPENING}})

4. **Narrative Voice**: Write in the specified narrative voice: {{TONE_VOICE}}

5. **Pacing**: Follow the {{TONE_PACING}} pacing style.

6. **Length**: Aim for 400-600 words. Be vivid but focused.

7. **NO QUESTIONS**: Do NOT end with a question or explicit prompt to the player. The message should feel like the natural start of a story, not a game prompt.

8. **Pure Narrative**: Write ONLY the narrative message itself. No preamble like "Here is the opening:" or meta-commentary. Start directly with the scene.

### Example Structure (adapt as needed):

- **Hook**: Open with something that captures attention (action, dialogue, vivid description, mood)
- **Ground the Reader**: Quickly establish where/when we are
- **Introduce Stakes**: Hint at tension, conflict, or the journey ahead
- **End with Forward Momentum**: Leave the scene at a natural pause that invites the player to engage

Remember: This is the player's introduction to the entire story. Make it compelling, atmospheric, and true to the blueprint's vision.
