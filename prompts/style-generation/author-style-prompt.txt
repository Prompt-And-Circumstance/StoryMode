### Field Guidelines

**id**: Use lowercase letters and underscores only. For authors: `firstname_lastname`. For styles: `descriptive_name`. For mashups: `author_style_mashup`.

**name**: The display name. For authors: full name. For styles: descriptive title. For mashups: creative combination name.

**category**: 1-3 relevant categories (e.g., "Horror", "Literary Fiction", "Romance", "Thriller", "Comic Fantasy").

**authorPrompt**: This is critical. Include:
1. Opening: "Write in a style inspired by [Name/Style]."
2. Sentence structure and rhythm
3. Narrative voice and POV tendencies
4. Descriptive approach (sparse/lush, sensory focus)
5. Dialogue characteristics
6. 4-6 distinctive techniques to employ (bullet points)
7. Atmosphere and mood guidance

**nsfwPrompt**: How this style handles romantic/mature content. What feels authentic vs out-of-place. Focus on emotional vs physical approach.

**keywords**: 5-8 searchable terms including author/work names, genre markers, techniques, mood descriptors.
